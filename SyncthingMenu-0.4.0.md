### Added
- Each folder in the Folders menu has its own submenu: Open in Finder, Rescan, and Pause or Resume.
- A Devices menu lists your other devices, each with Pause or Resume.
- Pause and caution marks show which folders and devices need a look, on the rows and on the menu items above them.

### Changed
- Rescan All and Pause All now live at the top of the Folders and Devices menus.
- The status line reads "All Paused" with a green dot while every device is paused; orange is reserved for problems.
